## Data and images

- Research and yield figures come from [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com)'s public APIs, captured by `scripts/collect-research.mjs` into a dated snapshot that names its source.
- Token and protocol icons are loaded from DefiLlama's icon service (`icons.llamao.fi`); none are committed here.

## Design

The workspace pages follow the layout of the team's earlier project Pilot, and their visual language is modelled on Royco's app. Harbor's palette follows the NEAR HACKATHON's visual language; see `design/harbor/README.md`.

## Coding agents

Harbor was written with coding agents, including Claude Code. See the README's section on reused work and AI-generated code.
